# Design System: Kushal Banda, GitHub profile

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Name on the Wall, Pressed Flat"**

This is kushalbanda.com's world (`kushalBanda/sites`, `docs/portfolio/DESIGN.md`) carried onto a page that strips CSS, scripts and web fonts. Every designed surface is a committed, self-contained SVG generated by `assets/build.py`, which is the token source: General Sans is embedded as a data URI in each file, images are inlined, and motion is CSS inside the SVG. Between the images, GitHub's own Markdown supplies the white body field, its own type, and its own link blue. The README does not try to restyle that field; it lets GitHub's page be the site's white page.

The order is the site's three fields: a grey name band with the name sliding off both edges, the white body (GitHub's), and an ink footer that the page curves into. The site's hover preview becomes a filmstrip of three product cards laid flat, each in its product's own colours, sitting directly under the band. Density is low and size carries the hierarchy: one family at weight 400, enormous for names, small for everything that supports them, with hairlines instead of boxes.

All sizes below are SVG user units in each file's viewBox. The images are fluid (`width="100%"` or a third of the column), so rendered size scales with GitHub's content column; a 1280-unit canvas renders at roughly 0.75 to 0.8 on desktop.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three fields in order: grey band, GitHub's page as the white body, ink footer with a curved top edge.
- Every designed surface is an SVG with embedded General Sans 400; no external fonts, images or services.
- Product colours live only inside product cards; blue #455CE9 appears once, on the footer's round button.
- Each image ships a desktop canvas and a phone canvas (`-sm`) switched by `<picture>` at max-width 600px; the footer also ships dark variants.
- Motion is SVG-native CSS, small and looping, and all of it stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Colors

The site's near-monochrome cool neutrals and single blue, plus three borrowed product palettes confined to their cards.

### Primary
- **Signal Blue** (blue): the footer's "Get in touch" circle, and nowhere else. GitHub's own link blue in the Markdown is the platform's, not this token.

### Tertiary (product palettes, card-only)
- **OpusBar Cream** (opusbar-cream) with **OpusBar Ink** (opusbar-ink) for the name and hairline and **OpusBar Quiet** (opusbar-quiet) for the kind line (5.56:1 on cream).
- **Graphy Blue** (graphy-blue) with paper for the name and hairline on desktop (display size, so 3.35:1 passes as large text), navy for the name on phones, and **Graphy Navy** (graphy-navy) for the kind line. Navy, not white, because white on Graphy blue measures 3.35:1, too low for small text; navy measures 4.92:1.
- **OpenTicker Ink** (openticker-ink) with **OpenTicker Paper** (openticker-paper) for the name, status pill and dot, and **OpenTicker Quiet** (openticker-quiet) for the kind line (7.03:1).

### Neutral
- **Portrait Grey** (grey): the name band field. White on it measures 4.58:1.
- **Ink** (ink): the location pill and the footer panel.
- **Paper** (paper): the sliding name, the role line, all text and strokes on grey and ink, and the Graphy card name on desktop.
- **Globe Grey** (globe-grey): the circle behind the line globe inside the location pill. Decorative only; it never carries text.
- **Quiet Grey on Ink** (muted-on-ink): the footer's email and site lines (6.84:1 on ink).
- **Hairline on Ink** (line-on-ink): the footer rule. Card hairlines use each card's own ink at 0.2 opacity.

### Named Rules
**The Three Fields Rule.** Grey band, then GitHub's page, then the ink footer, in that order. Nothing between the band and the footer is painted; the white body belongs to GitHub.

**The Cards Keep Their Colours Rule.** Product colours appear only inside their own card. They never become band, footer or button colours.

**The One Blue Rule.** One blue surface per README: the round contact button. Blue is never a card or band colour.

**The Contrast Over Brand Rule.** A product's text colour gives way when it fails on its own field at small size. Graphy's kind line is navy for this reason; extend the same test to any new card.

## Typography

**Display Font:** General Sans (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** General Sans inside SVGs; GitHub's system stack in the Markdown between them.

**Character:** The site's neutral grotesk at regular weight, so the giant name reads calm. Only the Regular 400 woff2 is embedded, once per SVG, via `@font-face`; the fallback stack is declared in every file.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, 250, 1, -0.03em): the sliding name only. Pinned with `textLength` 1418 so the loop is seamless whatever the renderer's metrics. Phones scale it to 0.64 (160) so a frame shows most of the name.
- **Headline** (400, 112, -0.03em; phones 96): "Let's work together" in the footer, two lines, the first indented.
- **Card Name** (400, 40, -0.03em; phones 64): product name in each card's foot.
- **Statement** (400, 38, 48 line step; phones 40): the role line on the band, two lines.
- **Pill** (400, 30, 40 line step): the city in the location pill, three short lines.
- **Meta** (400, 24; phones 30): footer email and site lines.
- **UI** (400, 22; phones 28): the round button label, centred.
- **Card Kind** (400, 21; phones 44): kind and year in each card's foot ("macOS menu bar app · 2026"); phones drop the year for a shorter kind ("Menu bar app"). The OpenTicker status label uses the same 20-to-21 step (phones 34, "Soon").

### Named Rules
**The Regular Weight Rule.** 400 everywhere. Size makes hierarchy, never weight.

**The Tight Giant Rule.** Anything at 40 units or more takes -0.03em tracking (written as absolute letter-spacing: -7.5 at 250, -3.4 at 112, -1.2 at 40). Smaller text keeps default tracking.

**The Phone Scale-Up Rule.** Phone canvases render cards at about a third of a narrow column, so text inside cards is set larger on `-sm` files (name 64, kind 44), never smaller.

## Layout

The README is a single GitHub column. Designed surfaces are full-width images or three inline images:

- **Name band:** 1280 by 460 (phones 720 by 600). Desktop puts the location pill top left (y 56), the role line top right (x 830), and the name along the bottom (baseline 432). Phones stack role on top, pill below it, then the name (baseline 560). The grey rect overdraws by 4 units on every side so no renderer shows a seam.
- **Filmstrip:** three linked cards in one `<p>`, 400 by 460 each with a 12-unit transparent gap baked into the right of the first two (412 wide), sized 33.6%, 33.6% and 32.6% so they sum to the column and share one scale. Content sits on a 28-unit inset; the hairline above the name is at y 360 (phones 318).
- **Markdown body:** two `###` headings, plain paragraphs, one `<sub>` stack line separated by middots, and the latest five Medium posts between `<!-- medium:start -->` markers with `<sub>` publication and date. This is GitHub's type and spacing; the system does not override it.
- **Footer:** 1280 by 640 (phones 720 by 780), side margin 80 (phones 48), hairline at y 470 (phones 460) with the round button centred on it, email and site under the rule, signature bottom right. A plain centred `<sub>` row of text links follows the image.

Responsive switching is `<picture>`: `<source media="(max-width: 600px)">` serves the `-sm` file. The footer orders four sources: phone dark, phone, dark, then the light desktop `<img>`.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from the fields and the curve, with two soft shadows inherited from the site and one translation for dark mode.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Screenshot** (`feDropShadow dx 0, dy 18, stdDeviation 20, #000 at 0.28`): the Graphy screenshot inside its card. The site's screenshot shadow, unchanged.
- **Page edge, light** (black ellipse matching the curve, `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation 22`, 0.45 opacity, clipped to the ink panel): GitHub's light page casting the site's soft shadow onto the footer.
- **Page edge, dark** (paper hairline along the curve, 1.5 wide, 0.18 opacity): on GitHub's dark page a shadow reads as mud, so the curve is drawn as a line instead.
- **Contact shadow** (ellipse 44 by 6 in opusbar-ink at 0.12): the ground under the running cat. Card-local.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Page Rule.** Only things that sit above a surface (a screenshot, the page edge, the cat) cast a shadow. Cards, pills and the band do not.

**The Dark Edge Rule.** In dark variants, depth that would be a shadow becomes a faint paper hairline.

## Shapes

The site's two silhouettes. The full circle: round button (r 95; phones 100), globe disc (r 52), avatar (96; phones 80, clipped circle). The full pill: OpenTicker's status pill (52 tall, rx 26; phones 80, rx 40) and the location pill, a half pill flush with the left edge (460 by 160, right end fully round). Cards and the screenshot take the 4-unit soft corner. Everything else is square and separated by 1-unit hairlines.

The footer's top edge is an elliptical arc (horizontal radius 0.75 of the canvas width; height 110, phones 70) cut where it meets the image sides. Above the arc the SVG is transparent, so GitHub's own page, light or dark, is the page that curves into the footer.

## Components

### Name band (signature)
The grey field with the name sliding left forever. Three copies of "Kushal Banda" with a short paper dash (0.55 of the font size, 0.07 tall) between them form one track that translates by one unit over 22s, linear, infinite.

### Location pill
Ink half-pill flush left: "Based / in Hyderabad, / India" in Pill type, then a globe-grey disc with a paper line globe (1.5 stroke) whose meridian flips with scaleX over 5s, linear, infinite.

### Role line
A drawn paper arrow (two 2-unit strokes, pointing down-right) above "AI Engineer / building tools for agents" in Statement type.

### Product cards
The site's hover preview, laid flat and linked. A 400 by 460 field in the product's colours with 4-unit corners, media in the upper part, a hairline in the card ink at 0.2, then Card Name and Card Kind.
- **OpusBar:** the oneko sprite at 4x, pixelated, alternating two run frames every 250ms (0.5s cycle), with a contact shadow.
- **Graphy:** a crop of the Explorer column with LineLens badges, inset 28, 4-unit corners on a #181818 backing, Screenshot shadow.
- **OpenTicker:** a centred outline status pill (openticker-paper at 0.24) with a dot pulsing to 0.25 opacity over 2s ease-in-out and "Coming soon" ("Soon" on phones).

### Footer panel
Ink below the curved edge: avatar beside the Headline, a full-width hairline, the round blue "Get in touch" circle centred on the hairline toward the right, Quiet-on-ink email and site, and the Tegaki "Kushal" signature in paper (4.6 stroke, round caps). Each signature stroke draws once via `stroke-dashoffset` on the expo-out ease cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), in the site's order, offset by 0.4s. The whole image is a single `mailto:` link.

### Motion
Everything is CSS inside the SVG: band slide (22s linear), globe meridian (5s linear), cat run (0.5s), status dot pulse (2s ease-in-out), signature draw (once, expo-out). Every file carries `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce){*{animation:none!important}}`. No script runs, so the build cannot react to scroll or visibility: the signature draws when the image loads, not when it scrolls into view.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** generate every designed surface from `assets/build.py` and commit the SVGs; edit tokens there, not in the output.
- **Do** embed General Sans 400 and every image as data URIs so each SVG renders alone.
- **Do** ship a `-sm` phone canvas for every image and switch with `<picture>` at max-width 600px; ship dark variants where a surface touches GitHub's page (the footer).
- **Do** keep the footer's area above the curve transparent so GitHub's page is the page that curves.
- **Do** set anything at 40 units or more in 400 at -0.03em, and set card text larger on phone canvases.
- **Do** check each card's text against its own field and change the text colour (as Graphy's navy kind line does) before shipping.
- **Do** stop every animation under prefers-reduced-motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add badge walls, stats cards, view counters or any third-party image service; the stack is one plain `<sub>` line.
- **Don't** put blue on anything but the footer's round button.
- **Don't** let product colours leave their cards.
- **Don't** lighten the band grey; white on #737677 is already close to the floor at 4.58:1.
- **Don't** use a second typeface or a bold weight inside the SVGs.
- **Don't** cast a shadow on GitHub's dark page; draw the hairline instead.
- **Don't** try to restyle GitHub's Markdown between the images; it is the white field.
